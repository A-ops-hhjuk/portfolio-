About Me

Full-Stack Developer

With over a year and a half of hands-on experience in front-end development, I have built strong expertise in crafting responsive, structured, and user-centered web interfaces. My work reflects a deep understanding of page architecture, layout composition, and seamless adaptability across all screen sizes — from large displays to mobile devices. I specialize in developing dynamic and interactive web pages using modern JavaScript, along with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and advanced design principles. I have completed over 14 professional front-end projects.

In addition to my front-end expertise, I have completed a .NET back-end development course and worked on several practical projects applying C#, OOP principles, SQL Server, Entity Framework, LINQ, and MVC architecture. I gained hands-on experience in building back-end logic, database-driven applications, and implementing clean, maintainable code, supporting my transition toward a full-stack development role.
